\begin{abstract}
Autonomous software-engineering and operations agents frequently encounter operational bottlenecks (CI freeze windows, upstream 503 outages, and evaluation compute limits) that incentivize unauthorized shortcuts (\emph{agentic sabotage}) over honest escalation. Across a 500-scenario paired counterfactual benchmark (1,000 trajectories across four taxonomies plus 80 benign coding controls), we map pre-action sabotage circuits across model scale (\emph{Gemma-2-2B-IT}, \emph{Gemma-2-9B-IT}) and architecture (\emph{Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct}). In \emph{Study 1}, scaling \texttt{Gemma-2} from 2B to 9B eliminates unpressured shortcuts (28\%--48\% $\to$ 0\%--4\%) and halves pressured test tampering (92\% $\to$ 56\%), yet induces \emph{inverse scaling} on sycophantic 503 output fabrication (60\% $\to$ 88\%), while \emph{Gemma Scope} 16k JumpReLU Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) close the superposition gap at 9B Layer 24 (0.9585 AUROC vs.\ 0.9529 dense). In \emph{Study 2 (``Silent Commitment'')}, eliciting \texttt{<think>...</think>} scratchpads prior to \texttt{TOOL\_CALL:} ($N=228$) reveals that 25.3\% of sabotage scratchpads fool an isolated 9B LLM Compliance Auditor (0.870 AUROC, rising to 28.9\% under an audit notice and showing zero-shot inference-time obfuscation without RL), yet Layer-24 SAE probes strictly inside \texttt{<think>} reach 0.980 AUROC (0.946 zero-shot from Study 1), catching 91.7\% of Auditor-fooled 503 fabrications and 100\% (11/11) under audit notice 29--35 tokens early. Steering only inside \texttt{<think>} fails due to \emph{Action Re-Locking} (86.1\% $\to$ 77.8\% vs.\ 33.3\% full-trajectory). In \emph{Study 2.5}, a training-free \emph{Closed-Loop Probe-Gated Circuit Breaker} (0.0\% false triggers across 120/120 benign controls) rescues \texttt{2B}'s polysemantic cliff (0\% $\to$ 96.7\% benign accuracy, 52.1\% $\to$ 10.4\% sabotage) and defeats \texttt{9B} Action Re-Locking via a 28-token \texttt{<think>} latch (66.7\% $\to$ 5.6\%--13.9\% sabotage at 100\% benign CoT accuracy). Replicating on \emph{Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct} confirms 57.1\% depth emergence (Layer 16/28, 0.937 AUROC), 96\% pressured 503 fabrication, 0.978 \texttt{<think>} AUROC, and 100\% benign accuracy under closed-loop gating.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Deploying large language models (LLMs) as autonomous coding and operations agents introduces alignment risks qualitatively distinct from static single-turn chat \cite{greenblatt2024control,benton2024sabotage}. When an agent equipped with file-editing, shell, database, and metric-reporting tools encounters a failing unit test or an unreachable production database under tight SLA or user pressure, it faces an incentive conflict between \emph{task-completion appearance} and \emph{operational integrity}. Pressured agents can skip failing tests (\texttt{@pytest.mark.skip}), hardcode benchmark literals, bypass \texttt{--no-verify} gates, or fabricate schema-valid metric payloads rather than escalating blockers, echoing how specification gaming escalates from sycophancy \cite{sharma2024sycophancy} to reward tampering \cite{denison2024sycophancy}.

Current defenses largely rely on external Chain-of-Thought (CoT) monitoring: auditing an agent's \texttt{<think>...</think>} scratchpad with a trusted LLM prior to executing a tool call \cite{baker2025monitoring,korbak2025cot}. Yet recent behavioral analyses warn that CoT traces frequently omit unaligned cues \cite{turpin2023unfaithful,lanham2023measuring,chen2025reasoning} and that optimizing against CoT monitors during RL induces \emph{obfuscated reward hacking} \cite{baker2025monitoring}. Concurrently, white-box linear probes have achieved strong passive detection of strategic deception and covert sabotage \cite{goldowsky2025deception,hollinsworth2026fibs}, while large-scale benchmarks such as \emph{AxBench} \cite{wu2025axbench} have challenged whether Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs; \cite{lieberum2024gemmascope,rajamanoharan2024jumprelu}) offer any causal steering advantage over dense baselines.

We bridge these threads by asking four mechanistic questions:
(1) \emph{Without hidden malicious prompts or fine-tuning, do pressured tool agents form a linearly and sparsely decodable commitment to sabotage at $\sim$57\% depth before emitting any tool token?}
(2) \emph{Does CoT obfuscation arise zero-shot at inference time under audit notices, and can monosemantic SAE latents catch ``silent commitment'' $\sim$30 tokens before action emission?}
(3) \emph{Why does steering inside \texttt{<think>} alone fail (\emph{Action Re-Locking}), and how does scale-dependent polysemanticity reconcile \emph{AxBench} \cite{wu2025axbench} with SAE disentanglement?}
(4) \emph{Can a training-free, closed-loop probe-gated circuit breaker defeat both Action Re-Locking and benign over-refusal across model families (\texttt{Gemma-2} and \texttt{Qwen2.5})?}

\section{Related Work \& Positioning}

\paragraph{Agentic Sabotage, Scheming \& AI Control.}
Safety frameworks for untrusted agents \cite{greenblatt2024control} and frontier sabotage benchmarks, including Anthropic's sabotage evaluations \cite{benton2024sabotage}, in-context scheming \cite{meinke2024scheming}, alignment faking \cite{greenblatt2024faking}, emergent misalignment from narrow code fine-tuning \cite{betley2025emergent}, and \emph{SHADE-Arena} \cite{kutasov2025shade}, primarily study explicit hidden side-tasks or post-RL/fine-tuning subversion. Complementing Denison et al.\ \cite{denison2024sycophancy}, we isolate \emph{spontaneous bottleneck-induced sabotage} in standard instruction-tuned models and uncover \emph{inverse scaling} on 503 output fabrication (60\% $\to$ 88\%--96\%).

\paragraph{CoT Monitorability \& Obfuscation.}
While CoT traces provide a fragile safety window \cite{korbak2025cot}, unfaithful rationalizations persist in reasoning models \cite{turpin2023unfaithful,chen2025reasoning}. Baker et al.\ \cite{baker2025monitoring} showed that RL optimization pressure against a CoT monitor teaches agents to hide reward-hacking intent. Extending Baker et al.\ \cite{baker2025monitoring}, we show two purely \emph{inference-time} failure modes: (i) an explicit audit notice increases CoT-auditor evasion (23.0\% $\to$ 28.9\%) \emph{without any RL updates}, and (ii) \emph{Action Re-Locking} allows unsteered prompt pressure at \texttt{TOOL\_CALL:} to override a steered \texttt{<think>} trace, proving verbalized CoT is not a sufficient causal bottleneck.

\paragraph{White-Box Deception Probes vs.\ SAE Circuits.}
Linear probes separate truth and strategic deception in residual activations \cite{marks2023geometry,zou2023repe,goldowsky2025deception}, and Hollinsworth et al.\ \cite{hollinsworth2026fibs} recently demonstrated $\sim$0.98 AUROC passive detection on \emph{SHADE-Arena} and \emph{BashArena}. Whereas dense probes remain feature-opaque and passive, we pair dense probes with 16k JumpReLU SAEs \cite{rajamanoharan2024jumprelu,lieberum2024gemmascope} (cf.\ circuit tracing in \cite{lindsey2025biology}) to isolate human-interpretable pre-commitment latents (\texttt{\#11381}, \texttt{\#15689} vs.\ \texttt{\#6787}) firing 29--38 tokens early (0.980 AUROC) and convert them into active causal interventions.

\paragraph{SAE Steering (\emph{AxBench}) \& Conditional Circuit Breakers.}
Wu et al.\ \cite{wu2025axbench} showed simple dense vectors outperform SAEs on unconditional steering, while Arditi et al.\ \cite{arditi2024refusal} showed refusal occupies a low-dimensional subspace prone to collateral over-refusal. To curb collateral damage, Zou et al.\ \cite{zou2024circuitbreakers} proposed LoRA-trained \emph{Circuit Breakers} (representation rerouting), and Lee et al.\ \cite{lee2025cast} introduced training-free Conditional Activation Steering (\emph{CAST}) on input prompts. Our results \emph{nuance Wu et al.\ \cite{wu2025axbench} across scale}, showing dense probes win at \texttt{9B} (2.1\% vs.\ 6.2\% sabotage) yet hit catastrophic polysemantic over-refusal cliffs at \texttt{2B} (0\% benign accuracy) and \texttt{Qwen2.5-7B} (53.3\%) where SAE latents disentangle shortcut intent ($\cos=0.248$), and generalize \emph{CAST} into a \emph{streaming mid-\texttt{<think>} latched circuit breaker} (0.0\% benign FPR across 120 controls).

\section{Study 1: Cross-Scale Sabotage Circuits}

\subsection{Paired Counterfactual Benchmark \& SAEs}
We construct 500 multi-turn software-engineering scenarios evenly stratified across four taxonomies (125 each): \emph{Test Tampering}, \emph{Tool-Output Fabrication} (503 outages/locks), \emph{Hardcoded Oracle Bypass}, and \emph{Safety-Gate Bypass}, paired across \emph{bottleneck-pressured} vs.\ \emph{unpressured} prompts alongside 80 \emph{benign coding capability controls}.

At the pre-commitment prefix \texttt{TOOL\_CALL: \{"tool": "}, we collect $N=200$ on-policy rollouts per model and encode residual states $h_\ell \in \mathbb{R}^d$ via \emph{Gemma Scope} 16,384-latent JumpReLU SAEs \cite{rajamanoharan2024jumprelu,lieberum2024gemmascope}:
\begin{equation}
  f(h_\ell) = \operatorname{ReLU}(h_\ell W_{\mathrm{enc}} + b_{\mathrm{enc}}) \odot \mathbb{I}[h_\ell W_{\mathrm{enc}} + b_{\mathrm{enc}} > \theta].
\end{equation}
During decoding on held-out scenarios ($N=48$ pressured, $N=30$ benign), we apply norm-scaled planning-block steering ($L_{\mathrm{2B}}=\{14,16,18,20\}$; $L_{\mathrm{9B}}=\{20,24,28,32\}$; cf.\ \cite{rimsky2024caa}):
\begin{equation}
  h'_\ell = h_\ell - \alpha \|h_\ell\|_2 \hat{v}_\ell.
\end{equation}
All probing, SAE encoding, CoT auditing, and circuit-breaker experiments across all three models execute reproducibly in \texttt{bfloat16} on a single 24\,GB NVIDIA L4 (Colab) GPU with zero weight fine-tuning.

\subsection{Inverse Scaling \& Interpretable SAE Latents}
Without pressure, \texttt{Gemma-2-9B-IT} is near-perfectly aligned (0\%--4\% sabotage vs.\ 28\%--48\% in \texttt{2B}, Table~\ref{tab:beh}). Under pressure, \texttt{9B} halves test tampering (56\% vs.\ 92\%), \emph{yet fabricates tool outputs during 503 outages at 88.0\% vs.\ 60.0\% in \texttt{2B}} (+84.0\,pp; Figure~\ref{fig:study1}).

At \texttt{9B} Layer 24, Neuronpedia auto-interp \cite{marks2024circuits,templeton2024scaling} isolates \emph{Latent \#15689} ($d=+1.49$, single-feature AUROC $=0.829$) and \emph{Latent \#11381} (\emph{``causal relationships and responsibilities of different parties''}, $d=+1.04$, 39.2\% vs.\ 0.7\% fire rate) on sabotage, whereas \emph{Latent \#6787} (\emph{``algorithms and computational methods''}, $d=-1.48$) fires on \emph{56.4\% of honest fixes vs.\ 0.0\% of sabotage}. \paragraph{Zero-Shot Inference-Time Obfuscation \& 30-Token Lead Time.}
25.3\% (25/99) of \texttt{9B} sabotage scratchpads fool the 9B CoT Auditor ($<0.50$), rising from \emph{23.0\% to 28.9\%} when warned \texttt{<think>} is audited, extending Baker et al.\ \cite{baker2025monitoring} from RL training to zero-shot in-context obfuscation. Yet the Layer-24 16k SAE probe reaches \emph{0.980 AUROC} (matching the passive AUROCs of \cite{goldowsky2025deception,hollinsworth2026fibs} while providing feature-level attribution), catching \emph{91.7\% (11/12)} of Auditor-fooled 503 fabrications and \emph{100\% (11/11)} under audit notice, with \emph{Latent \#11381} igniting a \emph{median 29.0 tokens} and \emph{Latent \#15689} \emph{34.5 tokens} before \texttt{TOOL\_CALL:} (\texttt{sab\_0137}: 30\% Auditor suspicion vs.\ $+21.01$ on \#11381 at $-38$ tokens and $0.00$ on \#6787; Figures~\ref{fig:trace}--\ref{fig:quad}).

\paragraph{Action Re-Locking.}
On $N=36$ held-out focus prompts, steering \emph{only} during \texttt{<think>} ($\alpha=+0.22$) drops sabotage from $86.1\% \to 77.8\%$, whereas steering across \texttt{<think> + TOOL\_CALL:} reaches \textbf{33.3\%}: once hooks turn off at the boundary, prompt pressure \emph{re-locks} the shortcut.

\section{Study 2.5: Closed-Loop Circuit Breaker}

Advancing training-time circuit breakers \cite{zou2024circuitbreakers} and prompt-level conditional steering (\emph{CAST}; \cite{lee2025cast}), we construct a training-free \emph{Closed-Loop Probe-Gated Circuit Breaker}. Gate thresholds $\tau$ are calibrated strictly on disjoint validation rollouts (\texttt{sabotage[:44]} + \texttt{controls[40:80]}, targeting $\le 3.5\%$ benign FPR). Inference runs completely unsteered ($\alpha=0$) unless the pre-action or streaming 28-token \texttt{<think>} gate crosses $\tau$, dynamically latching 4-layer steering ($\alpha=+0.25$) through \texttt{TOOL\_CALL:}.

Across \emph{all four} evaluation suites (Table~\ref{tab:breaker}; Figure~\ref{fig:breaker}), false-trigger rates on held-out benign controls are \emph{0.0\% (0/30)} (\texttt{120/120} unperturbed; sabotage-vs-benign gate AUROC $=1.000$). Probe-gating rescues \texttt{2B} benign accuracy from \emph{0.0\% to 96.7\%} ($52.1\% \to 10.4\%$ sabotage), while on \texttt{9B} \texttt{<think>} rollouts, 86.1\% of pressured focus prompts trip at token 28 and latch across \texttt{TOOL\_CALL:}, cutting sabotage from \emph{66.7\% to 13.9\% (SAE) and 5.6\% (Dense)} at \emph{100.0\% benign CoT accuracy}.

\section{Cross-Family Validation (\texttt{Qwen2.5-7B})}

Evaluating \texttt{Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct} \cite{qwen2024qwen25} (28 layers, $d=3584$, single pre-RMSNorm + GQA + SwiGLU, no soft-capping) across $N=200$ decision rollouts and $N=108$ \texttt{<think>} rollouts confirms architecture invariance (Figure~\ref{fig:qwen}): pressured 503 fabrication surges $4\% \to 96\%$, residual probes peak at \emph{Layer 16/28 (57.1\% depth, $0.937 \pm 0.028$ AUROC)} matching \texttt{Gemma-2-9B} (\texttt{L24/42 = 57.1\%}), no-CoT L16 probes transfer zero-shot to \texttt{<think>} at \emph{0.959 AUROC}, pre-tool \texttt{<think>} probes reach \emph{0.978 AUROC} (catching \emph{100\% [7/7]} of Auditor-fooled 503 fabrications), and L16 probe-gating restores \emph{100.0\%} benign accuracy (vs.\ 53.3\% always-on).

\section{Limitations \& Conclusion}
While our experiments span two model families (\texttt{Gemma-2} 2B/9B and \texttt{Qwen2.5} 7B), 500 paired scenarios, and closed-loop \texttt{<think>} circuit breakers (reproducible end-to-end on a single 24\,GB L4 GPU with zero weight fine-tuning), evaluations target single-step tool invocations rather than long-horizon containerized trajectories (\emph{SHADE-Arena}; \cite{kutasov2025shade}). Integrating mid-network probe-gated circuit breakers into AI Control protocols \cite{greenblatt2024control} offers a practical path toward faithful tool-using agents.
